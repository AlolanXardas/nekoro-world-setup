World rules for modpacks and servers, all set in config files: start commands and game rules, global datapacks, higher enchantment levels, mob spawn filters, recipe changes, water and lava behaviour, a wrapping world edge, void teleport, bone meal on more plants and world generation changes.
Made mainly for my Nekoro Pokemon modpack, but it works in any world.

Why this mod

This mod replaces several mods I used in my modpack. They are linked below, next to the feature that replaces them. Most of them probably do their job better than my version, so try them first.
I made my own so my modpack always has up-to-date mods with exactly what I need: things I was missing in the originals, and without the parts I don’t use.
Every feature can be turned off in the config. Apart from a fix for a world generation crash, everything starts off, so the mod does nothing until you turn something on.

Features

All files are in config/Nekoro/WorldSetup/ and every option is explained in the file.

start.json
• Game rules set when a world is created (modded game rules too), or again on every server start
• Commands run when the world is created, on every server start, on a player’s first join or on every join
Replaces World Start Commands and Global Gamerules
• Datapacks from config/Nekoro/WorldSetup/datapacks load in every world, new and existing
Replaces Paxi

world.json
• Higher maximum enchantment levels for the enchanting table, anvil, books and /enchant, with a different maximum per enchantment if you want, and an adjustable or removed “Too Expensive!” limit
• Enchantments that normally exclude each other can be combined (Sharpness with Smite, the Protections, Fortune with Silk Touch...), and enchantments can go on extra items, like Looting on axes
• Small enchantment rules: Feather Falling protects farmland, Infinity without arrows, more XP from Looting and Fortune, all Multishot arrows hit, shears and shields in the enchanting table
Replaces Universal Enchants
• Every Mending level above I repairs more per XP point
• Higher Silk Touch levels also work with my Nekoro Random Drop mod: rules with “silk_touch”: “bonus” get a higher chance with each level
• Digging sand, dirt or gravel next to water fills its place with water right away, so beaches and lakes don’t end up with water flowing everywhere. Built blocks are left out, so houses next to water can’t get flooded
Replaces Pretty Beaches
• Lava and water no longer make cobblestone or stone (basalt and obsidian can be turned off too)
Replaces Cobble Gen Haters
• Walking out of a square area takes players to the opposite side, like on a round planet
• Players who fall into the void appear at the top of the dimension and fall back down
• Bone meal also works on sugar cane, cactus, vines, nether wart, full melon and pumpkin stems, lily pads, small flowers, dead bushes, chorus, coral in warm oceans, spore blossoms, podzol, mycelium and dirt next to grass, each one with its own option
Replaces Universal Bone Meal

mobs.json
• Block mobs, or allow only the ones you list, by id, by whole mod or by tag
• Choose which kinds of spawning are checked (natural, spawners, structures, breeding, spawn eggs...)
• Optionally remove blocked mobs that already exist, and block mobs that appear without a normal spawn, like bees leaving hives
Replaces Bad Mobs

worldgen.json
• Flat bedrock floor and Nether roof
Replaces Flat Bedrock
• Remove ores from vanilla and mods, the big ore veins too, with a list of ores to keep
In my modpack this replaces Cave Spelunking: ores come from mining stone with my Nekoro Random Drop mod instead
• Remove cave tunnels and canyons, spaghetti and noodle caves, the big open caves (Terralith’s too), lava lakes, lava springs, the deep lava layer and geodes
Replaces No Caves Mod
• When biome mods place the same features in opposite orders, vanilla stops world generation with “Feature order cycle found”. This fix (on by default) lets the world generate and writes the features involved to the log

recipes.json
• Remove recipes by result, by recipe id, by mod or by tag
• Add recipes: slabs and stairs back to blocks, logs to sticks, logs to chests
Replaces the part of CraftTweaker I used (CraftTweaker can do far more)

/reload applies changes to world.json, mobs.json and recipes.json. start.json and worldgen.json need a restart, and world generation changes only affect new chunks.
